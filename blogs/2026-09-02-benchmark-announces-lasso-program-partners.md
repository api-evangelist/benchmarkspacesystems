Introducing the team forging the path to very low lunar orbit. Benchmark is advancing our Phase 1A/1B feasibility and design effort under DARPA's Lunar Assay via Small Satellite Orbiter (LASSO) program. LASSO evaluates options, then designs the integrated spacecraft systems and architectural readiness required to operate safely and persistently in very low lunar orbit, an environment that pushes propulsion, onboard navigation, and autonomous maneuvering to the edge of today's technical limits.
