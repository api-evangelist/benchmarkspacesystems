Meet Colin There are few on Earth who can match Colin’s flight hours when it comes to on-orbit software. As Avionics Architect, Colin brings deep, hard-won experience to designing the intelligence behind Benchmark’s modular propulsion controller, MAAVRIC. From the earliest phases of a program where broad thinking drives critical decisions on system architecture and communication protocols, to the final stages of qualification requiring an emphasis on execution and documentation, Colin is hands-on every step of the way.
